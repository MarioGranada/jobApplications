## About Wolt

At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. Founded in 2014 with restaurant food delivery; now delivery of (almost) everything in 500+ cities across 30 countries. Joined forces with DoorDash in 2022.

## The team

World-class customer service is a cornerstone of Wolt, backed by in-house built support tooling. The team builds several admin applications on top of Wolt's in-house chat application to provide a custom support platform that is scalable, reliable and efficient. The tool supports several thousand concurrent users on top of a chat application built to serve 20mil+ conversations each month. The aim is also to uncover and tackle issues before customers and partners notice.

## Key responsibilities

- Develop responsive and user-friendly interfaces using modern frameworks such as React + TypeScript
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate designs and requirements into functional features
- Ensure performance, scalability, and accessibility of web applications across browsers and devices
- Debug, test, and maintain codebases to ensure reliability and quality
- Stay updated with frontend technologies and implement best practices

Someone who has already shipped and run production software and can work independently.

- 4+ years of production experience in frontend development, with a strong focus on React.js and TypeScript
- Experience building scalable and efficient frontend applications

- Cross-team collaboration: work with multiple teams across Wolt, including on shared services, and coordinate changes with dependent teams
- Product thinking: join the conversation and challenge proposed solutions
- A supportive mindset: help colleagues grow through code reviews, pairing and knowledge sharing
- Curiosity and willingness to learn
