## Company

Wise is a global technology company, building the best way to move and manage the world's money. Min fees. Max ease. Full speed. Mission: make financial services faster, simpler and more accessible for everyone, while safeguarding the platform against financial crime.

## The team: Customer Risk Assessment

Building the centralized platform that gives Wise deep visibility into any customer and their associated financial crime (fincrime) risk. By tying together all fincrime topologies into a unified view, teams can make fast, accurate decisions. The mission: build out this centralized platform, tie complex risk signals together, and own the common components of risk mitigation, so Wise can scale compliance operations without compromising customer safety.

## How they work

Cross-functional team of backend and frontend engineers with Product Managers, Analysts and Designers. Values ownership, open feedback, stable and scalable systems, fast learning and iteration. Belief in automation, programmatic implementation and reusable design. Engineers are expected to understand underlying problems, challenge proposed solutions and propose simple, effective ones (not just work through Jira tickets).

## What you'll work on

- Build the full stack: design, develop and deploy resilient backend microservices in Java (Spring Boot) and build intuitive, accessible frontends using React and TypeScript
- Create a 360-degree risk view: centralized tools that aggregate data across multiple fincrime topologies
- Drive risk mitigation: own and develop common components of risk mitigation handling, with scalable, standardized workflows for Servicing Agents
- Scale operations: break down bottlenecks in servicing and compliance processes, delivering adaptable platform solutions other Wise product teams can evolve
- Take ownership: from identifying a problem and empathizing with stakeholders to an execution plan and shipping to production

## What you need

- Solid backend experience: strong engineering background ideally in Java (Spring Boot), with years of putting scalable microservices into production and maintaining them in a cloud-based environment
- Frontend skills: performant, responsive web applications using React and TypeScript/JavaScript
- High coding standards: clean, scalable, testable code; test coverage best practices, the test pyramid, the value of code reviews
- Systems architecture knowledge: designing and implementing distributed, concurrent systems; tradeoffs between stateful/stateless and synchronous/asynchronous architectures
- Product mindset: break complex projects into incremental milestones that deliver customer value
- Great communication: explain technical concepts to non-technical or distributed cross-functional peers
