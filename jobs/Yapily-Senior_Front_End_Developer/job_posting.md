## About Yapily

Open banking infrastructure platform. Securely connects companies, from growth to enterprise, to thousands of banks worldwide so they can access data and initiate payments.

## Role overview

Architect the front-end ecosystem and guide the team into an AI-native future. Hands-on development plus leadership: mentor the front-end team in adopting AI-native workflows, champion agentic coding workflows, influence the product roadmap, and make sure the UI components (the primary interface for clients) are built for scalability, security and velocity. Aside from the API, these UI components are the main interaction clients and their customers have with Yapily. Bridge between engineering and product.

Stack: React, Playwright, Vite, Node.js, Java, Spring. Aggregation businesses or open banking experience is highly advantageous.

## Key tasks

- Lead and mentor the Front End team; drive technical excellence and AI-native development workflows
- Architect and oversee the front-end ecosystem: UI strategy, accessibility, scalability
- Liaise with backend and product for architectural alignment
- Architect and build complex, high-performance SPAs; champion agentic coding workflows
- Define technical standards and code review processes; security and performance optimization
- Partner with Product on the technical roadmap
- Lead cross-functional initiatives across design, backend and product
- Application security best practices and regular audits
- Own QA: testing strategies and automation

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's in Computer Science, Engineering or related; postgraduate preferred
- Software development and architecture experience, preferably fintech or banking
- Complex projects across frontend and backend
- Strong leadership in high-performing engineering teams
- Exceptional technical and non-technical communication
- Understanding of Open Banking trends and regulatory requirements
