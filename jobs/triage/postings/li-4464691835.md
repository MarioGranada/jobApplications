Senior React & TypeScript Engineer at BairesDev

As a Senior React & TypeScript Engineer, you will lead the development of highly responsive and robust web applications. You will craft high-performance frontend solutions, establishing solid architectures and ensuring clean, maintainable code structures across our digital platforms.

What You'll Do


 Architect and build scalable, accessible user interfaces using React and TypeScript.
 Design modular, reusable frontend components and manage complex application states.
 Collaborate with designers and backend teams to integrate reliable APIs and data streams.
 Implement comprehensive frontend testing strategies to ensure high performance and responsiveness.
 Perform code reviews and champion modern development standards and best practices.


What We Are Looking For


 5+ years of experience in Frontend Development or a related field.
 Proven expertise in React and TypeScript.
 Strong hands-on experience with state management and component architecture.
 Solid understanding of REST and GraphQL API integrations.
 Experience with unit and frontend testing, including performance and accessibility.
 Advanced proficiency in English.
