About the job

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Design Engineer based in Spain.

As a Design Engineer, you will shape the visual identity and user experience of a high-impact technology website, translating complex technical products into engaging, intuitive digital experiences. You will combine strong visual design skills with hands-on frontend development, taking projects from early concepts and prototypes through implementation and launch. Working across Design, Marketing, and Growth, you will create compelling website experiences that help developers and business teams understand the value of advanced device intelligence technology. You will contribute to a cohesive design system, build interactive demonstrations, and continuously refine performance, accessibility, and usability. This role offers significant creative ownership within a globally distributed, remote-first engineering environment that embraces experimentation and AI-powered workflows.

Accountabilities

You will own the design and implementation of key website experiences, balancing creative excellence with technical quality and business objectives. Your responsibilities will include:


Lead the design and development of core website experiences, including the homepage, product pages, pricing pages, and navigation, from initial exploration through responsive implementation and launch. 
Translate complex technical product concepts into clear visual explanations, engaging interfaces, and interactive demonstrations that help users understand and evaluate the platform. 
Refine typography, visual hierarchy, composition, motion, and interaction details to create a distinctive and consistent experience across devices. 
Build and maintain reusable components, design tokens, and page patterns that connect the visual design system with production code. 
Develop accessible, mobile-friendly, high-performance website experiences that follow modern web standards and work consistently across browsers. 
Collaborate with Design, Marketing, Growth, and engineering partners on product launches, website experiments, and conversion-focused initiatives. 
Take projects through design reviews, testing, release, and ongoing optimization, using user feedback and website performance data to improve usability and conversion. 
Share ideas proactively, develop prototypes, and make thoughtful decisions that balance creative ambition, technical feasibility, and delivery timelines. 
Explore and implement AI-powered workflows, including machine-readable design specifications, reusable skills, and internal tools that accelerate creative and development processes. 
Help evolve the website's design system and identify opportunities to improve collaboration, consistency, and production efficiency. 


Requirements

The ideal candidate combines exceptional visual design judgment with strong frontend engineering capabilities and a proven ability to deliver polished digital experiences independently. You should be comfortable moving between design tools and code, experimenting with new technologies, and collaborating with distributed teams. Key qualifications include:


6–10 years of relevant experience designing and building websites or interactive digital experiences. 
A track record of owning projects end to end as a hands-on individual contributor. 
An active portfolio website, GitHub profile, or relevant social profile showcasing high-quality websites and interactive experiences, with a clear indication of your personal contributions. 
Strong visual and interaction design fundamentals, including typography, hierarchy, spacing, composition, and responsive layouts. 
Advanced proficiency in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and TypeScript. 
Experience building production websites with React and modern web frameworks such as Astro. 
Hands-on experience with canvas animations and shaders, and the ability to prototype motion and interactions directly in the browser. 
Proficiency in Figma and experience exploring design concepts through both visual tools and code. 
Familiarity with AI-assisted coding tools and the ability to use AI-first workflows to accelerate design exploration and implementation. 
Practical knowledge of web performance, accessibility standards, and browser behavior, including the ability to diagnose and resolve technical issues. 
Ability to translate open-ended briefs into finished digital experiences, explain design and technical decisions, and incorporate feedback from different disciplines. 
Strong initiative, independent problem-solving skills, and clear communication in a fully distributed team. 
Proficiency with macOS and comfort collaborating remotely using tools such as Slack, Google Workspace, and Zoom. 


Additional Experience That Would Be Advantageous


Experience with three.js, WebGL, motion.dev, Blender, or other 3D and animation technologies. 
Background working on technical B2B products. 
Experience with Astro and interactive data visualization.
