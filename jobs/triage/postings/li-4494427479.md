About the job

About the company:

KIEFER is building Greece's integrated AI ecosystem. From renewable energy infrastructure and AI systems to robotics and enterprise applications, we connect the technologies that power Europe's intelligent future. Founded in 2014, KIEFER has delivered 600MW+ of energy projects and is now developing sovereign AI infrastructure, enterprise AI products and physical AI systems for Greece and Southeast Europe.

About The Role

Hyperion-X is our trading platform for renewable energy portfolios on the Greek market: forecasting, scheduling, bidding, and integrations with the market and system operators. We are looking for a senior engineer to own it end to end with a backend focus, from the data model to its behaviour in production. You turn trading prototypes and data-science algorithms into fast, reliable production features, working directly with the business side, customers and a small engineering team.

What You Will Do


Build and run Hyperion-X features end to end, from the data model to production, including their tests and security. 
Own the integrations with energy market and system operator APIs, and the data pipelines behind them. 
Turn prototypes and algorithms from trading and data science into fast, tested production code. 
Run the service's releases, deploys and incidents on Kubernetes, with Platform Engineering behind you. 
Shape new features with customers and the business side, from a vague request to an RFC, keeping everyone up to date. 
Work through AI agents with review, and keep standards and decisions written down. 


What You Will Need


6+ years of professional software engineering with backend as your core depth, including typed Python in production (FastAPI or similar) and performance work with NumPy and pandas. 
Strong system and API design: REST APIs, hands-on experience with monoliths and microservices and when each fits, extension without rewrites. 
Solid data modelling and hands-on SQL, relational and NoSQL: normalization, indexing, query plans and performance. 
Asynchronous systems: queue-based data flows in production; you choose between synchronous calls, queues and event streams and make them resilient (idempotency, retries, circuit breakers, backpressure). 
You own what you ship: CI/CD, Kubernetes, observability, test strategy, performance and security by design. 
You have taken others' prototypes or algorithms into production with tests against the reference, and built reliable time-series integrations with correct units and time. 
Working experience with React and TypeScript, including frontend testing, and a basic understanding of web client architecture, enough to ship a web feature end to end. 
You direct AI agents, verify their work and stay accountable for design and release decisions. 
Strong communication in fluent English: you have worked directly with customers or business stakeholders. 
You are an EU citizen, or you live in the EU with the right to work there. 


Nice to have


Experience shipping apps end to end. 
Event streaming such as Kafka, infrastructure as code, or hands-on experience with our stack. 
Energy markets, trading or forecasting (XGBoost, scikit-learn); SOAP and mutual TLS. 
Greek language. 


What Is There For You


Remote within the EU (CET working hours) or hybrid from Athens. 
28 weekdays of paid annual leave (in Greece). 
Compensation benchmarked against the Greek and EU market, private health insurance or the remote-EU equivalent. 
Real ownership of a product with direct business impact. 
Budget for conferences, courses and certifications.
