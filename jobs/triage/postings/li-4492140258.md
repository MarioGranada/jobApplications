About the job

Looking for

A product-minded engineer to build AI-powered products for banks and fintechs. You will decide what we build, build it and own how it does with real clients.




About Triple

Triple is building the intelligence layer for financial services. From our roots in merchant transaction data, we’ve expanded to solve one of the industry’s biggest challenges: payment disputes. We deploy AI agents that automate them for top-tier card issuers.




With $10M in funding and a global footprint spanning over 60 banks and fintechs (including Airwallex, Qonto and Trade Republic), we are a fast-moving, experienced team.




What you will do

Own whole product areas, from the problem a bank has to the shipped feature and the numbers that show it worked: recovery rates, time per case and where cases stall.
Decide what we build next. Work with banks, analysts and the cases themselves to find the problems worth solving, and turn them into product.
Make product and architecture calls that still hold when we run disputes for many more banks and fintechs.
Design how disputes run on AI agents: what the agent decides, where a person steps in, and how the bank sees that each decision followed the scheme rules.
Prototype fast, put it in front of real users, and cut what does not work.
Build across the whole stack yourself: domain model, APIs, agent flows, and the interfaces analysts and cardholders use.
Work AI-first, as all of Triple does. We use and build our own agent tooling every day: coding and QA harnesses, knowledge graphs, synthetic customers.




What you need

6+ years building production software, including at least one product area you took from an idea to something customers depend on.
Strong full-stack engineering: you can build a whole feature alone, from schema and API to agent flow and interface. Strong TypeScript; Go, Python or another backend language is a plus. Today we run TypeScript, Next.js, Postgres and Temporal on Google Cloud, and that will change as we scale.
Product judgment you can point to: features you proposed, built and measured, and ideas you dropped.
You think beyond the next ticket. You can say where a product should be in two years and what to build this month to get there.
You have built B2B products that other companies, ideally financial institutions, run their operations on.
You have shipped LLM features to production and designed the product around where they fail.
You communicate clearly, to engineers and to the people who run dispute operations.
You move fast and verify what matters. Where money, deadlines or client data are involved, you prove it works before it ships.
English proficiency is required. Spanish is a plus.




Conditions

100% remote. Competitive salary, and equity. We are an AI-native company: every engineer here works with coding agents daily, and you will have the AI tools you need without usage limits. We build client-led, which means you will sit close to the banks using what you ship. The chance to shape how the world's largest banks run operations with AI.
