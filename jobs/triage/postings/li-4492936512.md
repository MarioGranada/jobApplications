About the job

Senior Full Stack Engineer (x4) | Series A | AI Agents

Remote | Europe or LATAM | $70K–$110K + Equity




Already building AI products but want to work on bigger problems, with more responsibility for the technical decisions? Read on!




I'm hiring x4 Senior Full Stack Engineers for a Series A AI startup with $30M+ raised, $6M+ ARR and a team of around 15 engineers.




Their technology has already handled hundreds of millions of customer conversations for hundreds of brands. Despite that scale, the engineering team is still small enough for you to have a real say in what gets built and how.




What does the product do?

Most customer service chatbots can answer simple questions. But ask for a refund, report a missing order or need something more complicated, and you'll usually end up speaking to a human.




This company builds AI agents that can resolve those problems without human help.




The agents connect to customer, order and product systems, allowing them to understand requests and take actions such as issuing refunds, tracking orders and recommending products.




The longer-term goal is to build agents that work across customer support, shopping, social media and voice.




What will you work on?

You'll build and improve the systems behind these AI agents, including LLM orchestration, RAG, evaluations, tool integrations and infrastructure that needs to work reliably at scale.




You'll own features from technical design through to production, rather than working on just one part of a larger system.




You'll join a remote engineering team with backgrounds at Salesforce/MuleSoft, BrowserStack and Fireflies.ai, where engineers have the freedom to make technical decisions and move quickly.




Tech stack: TypeScript, Node.js/Express, React/Next.js, AWS, PostgreSQL, Redis + Python.




What they're looking for

5+ years of software engineering experience, ideally in startups or scale-ups
Strong TypeScript, Node.js, React and AWS experience
Solid backend architecture and distributed systems knowledge
Experience building and shipping LLM-powered products to real users, ideally involving agents, RAG or evaluations
Experience owning features from design through to production
Regular use of AI coding tools such as Claude Code or Cursor, with the ability to test and review the code they produce

Previous commercial experience building production LLM systems is essential.




$70K–$110K + equity | Fully remote Europe/LATAM | B2B or EOR via Deel
