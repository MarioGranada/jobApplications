About this role.

About Nebius:

Nebius is leading a new era in cloud infrastructure for the global AI economy. We are building a full-stack AI cloud platform that supports developers and enterprises from data and model training through to production deployment, without the cost and complexity of building large in-house AI/ML infrastructure.

Built by engineers, for engineers. From large-scale GPU orchestration to inference optimization, we own the hard problems across compute, storage, networking and applied AI.

Listed on Nasdaq (NBIS) and headquartered in Amsterdam, we have a global footprint with R&D hubs across Europe, the UK, North America and Israel. Our team of 1,500+ includes hundreds of engineers with deep expertise across hardware, software and AI R&D.

The role

We are looking for Senior Fullstack Engineers to work on creating UX solutions for external and internal customers. Thousands of AI engineers across healthcare, robotics, media, and retail as well as your own collegues interact with Nebius through what you build.

Your responsibilities:

Working with multiple internal customers, while keeping UX consistent across all the different services
Working with APIs in multiple data centers, with changing regional model, while thinking about client latencies
Creating completely new UX solutions, not directly related to infrastructure services, such as customer communication services, Cloud shell, IaaC code generation and anything else that will improve customer experience
Creating complex user flow scenarios for different customer tiers in close collaboration with other teams
Building a design system that stays coherent as the product surface doubles. And making instruments to facilitate quick delivery across development, design and documentation teams

We expect you to have:

5+ years of experience in software development.
Strong proficiency in modern web development
Strong proficiency in Node.JS
Understanding of modern databases (PostgreSQL or other relational databases)
Experience designing and working with APIs (RESTful and other RPC)

It would be nice if you have:

Experience with React, tanstack-query
Experience with NestJS or similar Node.js frameworks.
Exposure to DevOps concepts (Docker, Kubernetes, Linux).
Experience with Go development
Experience with modern message queues/brokers systems
Experience working with distributed systems or complex workflows.

We conduct coding interviews as part of the process.
