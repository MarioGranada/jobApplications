Job Description

We are looking for a Full-Stack Engineer (CIAM) with identity and access management experience. This engineer has UI expertise to help develop customer, distributor, and supplier-facing digital platforms at Rockwell Automation.

You will have hands-on experience in front and backend development, API integration, cloud platform, and modern software engineering practices.

We are looking for collaboration with multiple teams, including architects, security analysts, business analysts, product owners, UX/UI designers, QA engineers, DB Administrators, and DevOps teams. The goal of this collaboration is to deliver high quality digital solutions that meet business and technical requirements.

Main Responsibilities


Experience with IAM concepts around Authentication, Authorization, Federation, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) and policy-based access models
Implement modern UI/UX designs and integration with enterprise design systems.
Build reusable UI components with react.js, next.js and JavaScript/typescripts.
Build responsive, user-friendly and accessible UI components supporting customer management portals.
Integrate IAM solutions like Okta, Auth0, Azure AD with applications supporting login, access, authentication, and attribute-based authorization models
Develop and integrate RESTful APIs and microservices architecture components.
Hands-on knowledge of OAuth 2.0, OIDC, SAML, JWT, MFA, and federation concepts.
Implement authentication, authorization, session management, and application security.
Perform application troubleshooting, debugging, and performance optimization.
Collaborate with UX designers to implement and design systems across digital properties
Participate in CI/CD processes and Azure cloud container deployments
Follow secure coding practices aligned with enterprise IT standards.
