## About 011h

Industrialized, sustainable construction (timber buildings). "The first end-to-end digital platform for construction in Europe." Operates in Spain, planning to expand internationally.

## The role

Product Engineer specialized in frontend (React, TypeScript) who also understands product and architecture as a whole. Leads the evolution of the frontend experience, the Design System and app architecture. Joins a four-person Engineering team working closely with Product and Design. Not ticket implementers: engineers with technical and product judgment.

## What you'll do

- Build product end to end: from the business problem to deploying and measuring impact
- Complex, accessible, consistent, performant interfaces in React and TypeScript
- Evolve the Design System: component library, interaction patterns, visual standards
- Understand backend domain modelling, microservices and frontend/backend contracts
- DDD, hexagonal architecture, event-driven services
- Resilient solutions: loading, errors, data consistency, async, edge cases
- Work with Product and Design from early definition; build functional prototypes
- AI-native engineering: work daily with coding agents like Claude Code (skills, instructions, context, workflows); set up the agents' environment, orchestrate tasks, build feedback loops
- Own the software: critically evaluate AI-generated code; testing, automation, CI, observability
- Testing strategies and automated quality checks
- Continuously improve how the team works
