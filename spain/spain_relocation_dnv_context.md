## 2. Employment Situation & Strategy (Post-Layoff)

* **Current Situation:** Recently laid off from **Coursy** (the original Estonian employer that sponsored the DNV application).
* **Visa Validity Impact:**
  * The Type D visa is **not** automatically cancelled and remains valid for entry into Spain in January 2027.
  * However, the A1 and S1 certificates tied to Coursy lose their underlying employment basis upon deregistration from Estonia's Employment Register (*TÖR*).
  * Under Spanish immigration rules (Law 14/2013), material changes to DNV conditions must be reported to **UGE-CE** within **30 days**, and qualifying conditions must be active well ahead of the **July 2027 3-year extension**.
* **Target Employment Tracks (Both Open):**
  1. **Primary / Preferred Track — New Remote Employee Role (Company Outside Spain):**
     * Must be a bona fide employment contract with a non-Spanish company active for at least 1 year, with written authorization to work remotely from Spain.
     * **2026/2027 Minimum Gross Income Threshold:** €2,849/month (200% of 2026 SMI) for primary applicant + €1,068/month (75% SMI) for spouse = **€3,917/month gross total**.
     * **Advantage:** No 3-month tenure requirement once already holding an approved DNV; preserves clean **Beckham Law (24% flat tax)** eligibility; if hired by an EU/Estonian company, allows a fresh **A1/S1 certificate** (retaining Estonian social security and 1st/2nd pension pillar contributions).
  2. **Secondary Track — Local Job Offer from a Spanish Company:**
     * The DNV (`COLELETEL`) does **not** allow direct employment with a Spanish company without modifying the permit.
     * Requires filing a formal permit modification **before** starting work—ideally as a **Highly Qualified Professional (*Profesional Altamente Cualificado* / PAC)** via UGE-CE (20-working-day resolution) or a general *Cuenta Ajena* modification.
     * Preserves **Beckham Law (Modelo 149)** eligibility if filed within 6 months of Spanish Social Security registration.

* [ ] **Step 6 — UGE-CE Notification / Modification:** Work with a Spanish immigration lawyer to submit the new foreign employment contract to UGE-CE (or file a PAC/*Cuenta Ajena* modification if taking a Spanish job).

* [ ] **2027–2028 Compliance:** Keep absences from Spain strictly under **90 days total** across the 24-month period; take and pass the **CCSE exam** via Instituto Cervantes (exempt from DELE A2 language exam as Colombian nationals).
* [ ] **January 2029 (2-Year Mark — Article 22 Fast-Track):** Submit **Spanish Citizenship by Residence (*Nacionalidad Española por Residencia*)** application with apostilled Colombian criminal record (<90 days old), CCSE certificate, full passport scans, and Modelo 790-026. Dual Colombian–Spanish citizenship is preserved.
