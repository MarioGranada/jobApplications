# Spanish digital nomad visa (DNV): requirements and procedure, Spanish Embassy in Tallinn

- Source: [Visados Nacionales – Visado de residencia para teletrabajo (nómada digital)](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Embajadas/tallin/es/ServiciosConsulares/Paginas/index.aspx?scco=Estonia&scd=272&scca=Visados&scs=Visados+Nacionales+-+Visado+de+residencia+para+teletrabajo+(n%c3%b3mada+digital))
- Read on 2026-11-23. The page shows no last-updated date. Check the current list and fee with the embassy before relying on it.
- Scope: this is the **initial visa application at the consulate**. In-country changes (notifying a new employer, changing permit) and the 3-year extension go through **UGE-CE**, not the embassy (see `spain_relocation_dnv_context.md`).

## 1. General information

- For foreigners who **work remotely for companies located outside Spain**, using only computer and telecommunication means.
- **Employees (*cuenta ajena*):** may work **only for companies outside Spain**.
- **Self-employed (*cuenta propia*):** may also work for Spanish companies, up to **20% of their total activity**.
- **Qualification:** a graduate or postgraduate degree from a prestigious university, vocational training centre or business school, **or** at least **3 years of proven professional experience**.
- **Eligible family members:**
  - spouse or registered partner
  - children under 18, and older children who depend economically on the teleworker and haven't formed their own family unit
  - dependent ascendants (parents or grandparents)
- A **NIE** must be requested at the same time as the visa.

## 2. Required documents

Bring the original and a copy of each.

### 2.1 General (teleworker and each family member)

1. **National visa application form** (*Solicitud de visado nacional*), filled in and signed by each applicant or their representative. [PDF form](http://www.exteriores.gob.es/DocumentosAuxiliaresSC/Estonia/TALLIN%20(E)/Impreso%20solicitud%20visado%20nacional%20espa%C3%B1ol.pdf)
2. **Photo:** recent, passport size, colour, light background, taken from the front, no dark glasses, no glare, nothing covering the face oval.
3. **Valid passport:** original plus a copy of the biometric data page(s). At least 1 year of validity and two blank pages. Passports issued more than 10 years ago aren't accepted.
4. **Criminal record certificate** (adults), issued within the last 2 years by the country or countries of residence, **plus** a sworn statement (*declaración responsable*) of no criminal record in the last 5 years.
5. **Proof of legal residence in the consular district:** the Estonian residence card.
6. **Representative** (only if applying through one): a copy of their ID or passport and of the notarial power of attorney or other proof of representation. Show the originals at submission.
7. **Visa fee:** €90 (2026), paid in cash or by card.
8. **Health insurance:** a certificate from a public or private insurer authorised in Spain and listed by the DGSFP, covering every risk the Spanish public health system covers.
   - Not needed if coverage comes through Spanish Social Security.
   - If a social security agreement applies, bring instead a *certificado de derecho de cobertura* from the home country's competent institution.

### 2.2 Specific to the teleworker

1. **Company certificate:**
   - **Employee:** at least **3 months** at the company, and the company's **express authorisation to work remotely**.
   - **Self-employed:** a contractual relationship of at least **3 months**, and the terms under which the remote work will be done.
2. **Commercial registry certificate** (or equivalent) showing the company's **incorporation date (at least 1 year old)** and its type of activity.
3. **Sworn statement** (*declaración responsable*) from the company or the self-employed person committing to meet **social security obligations before work starts**.
4. **Employees:** proof that the **company applied to register with Spanish Social Security**, and proof of the **employee's enrolment** in it.
5. **Self-employed:** proof of enrolment in **RETA** (the self-employed regime).
6. **Alternative to items 4 and 5:** keep the home country's social security through a bilateral social security agreement. The home country issues a *certificado de legislación aplicable* for teleworkers. Only some countries issue it, and the sworn statement must mention it.
   - [Social security agreements](https://www.seg-social.es/wps/portal/wss/internet/InformacionUtil/32078/32253)
   - [Model certificates under bilateral agreements](https://www.seg-social.es/wps/portal/wss/internet/InformacionUtil/32078/38626/38627)
7. **Economic resources** (monthly):
   - **Teleworker:** 200% of the SMI (*salario mínimo interprofesional*, Spain's minimum wage).
   - **Family:** at least 75% of the SMI for the first family member, plus 25% of the SMI for each additional one.
   - Any evidence is accepted: an employment contract, a firm job offer, or a commercial contract (self-employed). Ownership, legality and availability of the funds must be shown.
8. **Degree or experience:** a graduate or postgraduate degree from a prestigious university, vocational training centre or business school, **or** at least 3 years of comparable professional experience.
   - Regulated professions need the degree homologated.
   - If a vocational qualification's equivalence is doubtful, the competent education authority may have to validate it.
   - The visa **isn't granted** if the conditions match an intra-company transfer visa (*traslado intraempresarial*).

### 2.3 Specific to family members

1. **Proof of relationship:** civil registry birth or marriage certificates, a registered partnership certificate, or any document proving an unregistered partnership.
2. **Adult children:** proof of economic dependence and marital status.
3. **Ascendants:** proof that they depend on the teleworker.

## 3. Procedure

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Competent office | The Tallinn consular office, for people who live in its consular district |
| Who submits | The applicant in person (a parent for minors), or a duly authorised representative |
| Appointment | By email to **emb.tallin.sc@maec.es** |
| Address and hours | Liivalaia 13, 10118 Tallinn · Monday to Friday, 9:00–13:00 |
| Receipt | A receipt with a code to track the file at [sutramiteconsular.maec.es](https://sutramiteconsular.maec.es/Home.aspx) |
| Extra documents | The office may ask for missing or additional documents or data, or call the applicant to an interview |
| Decision | **10 days** from the day after submission. Longer if extra documents are requested or an interview is held. |
| Collection | In person or by a representative, without appointment, within **1 month** of the day after the approval is notified |
| Refusal | Always notified in writing, with reasons |
| Appeals | (a) *Recurso de reposición* to the consular office within 1 month, or (b) *recurso contencioso-administrativo* before the Tribunal Superior de Justicia de Madrid within 2 months |

## 4. Validity

- **1 year**, or the length of the residence authorisation if that is shorter.
- The visa itself proves residence in Spain while it's valid. A TIE (*Tarjeta de Identidad de Extranjero*) isn't required, but can be requested at the Oficina de Extranjeros or the relevant police station.

## 5. What this means for Mario's case

- **Employer must be non-Spanish:** confirms that a Spanish entity or Spanish EOR doesn't fit the employee DNV.
- **Social security:** an employee needs either the employer's Spanish Social Security registration (item 2.2.4) or a home-country *certificado de legislación aplicable* (item 2.2.6). That's why a Bulgarian employer without an A1 must register in Spain, or agree that Mario fulfils its obligations.
- **What a new employer would provide** (mirrors items 2.2.1–2.2.3): a remote-work authorisation letter, a commercial registry extract showing at least 1 year of existence, and the social security sworn statement.
- **The 3-month seniority** applies to the initial application. Mario already holds the visa, so a new employer is notified to UGE-CE instead (see `spain_relocation_dnv_context.md`).
- **Income threshold:** 200% SMI for Mario plus 75% SMI for his spouse (€3,917/month gross in total for 2026/2027, per the context file).
